About ALI GLOBAL TRAVEL & TOURS

Your Trusted Travel Partner in Malaysia

Licensed Inbound Specialist

Dedicated to Showing You the Very Best of Malaysia

ALI GLOBAL TRAVEL & TOURS SDN. BHD. is a MOTAC-licensed travel agency based in Bandar Baru Salak Tinggi, Sepang, Selangor, Malaysia.

As an inbound tour specialist, we help international and local guests explore Malaysia through customised tour packages, sightseeing trips and transportation arrangements.

From airport pickups to multi-day group tours, we arrange trips according to our guests' travel plans, group sizes and interests.

Our goal is to make every journey convenient, comfortable and memorable.
